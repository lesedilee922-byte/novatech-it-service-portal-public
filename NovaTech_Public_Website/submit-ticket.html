Before you submit

  • Log one main issue per ticket.
  • Include the affected device or Asset ID where known.
  • Describe impact, urgency, and any error message.
  • Do not include passwords or sensitive personal data.

Support-level response targets

Level 1Within 2 hours
Level 2Within 24 hours
Level 3Within 48 hours*

These are response-time targets from the Task 1 SOP, not guaranteed resolution times. Level 3 may take longer for highly complex cases. The support team assigns priority after assessing impact and urgency.

How tickets are handled

The support team logs and triages the request, assigns priority P1–P4, attempts Level 1 resolution, escalates if required, communicates progress, and closes the ticket after resolution confirmation.
